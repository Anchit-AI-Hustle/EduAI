# EveryYear (`ai-school-full-stack`)

An AI-first, self-evolving school and skill-graph platform covering a learner's whole education:
- **Early Years (0-6):** Home learning (0-3) grounded in [MWCD Navchetana 2024](https://spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/early-childhood-care-and-education/documents) with zero child screen exposure ([IAP Screen Time Guidelines](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34969943/)), and Nursery, LKG, UKG grounded in [MWCD Aadharshila 2024](https://wcd.gov.in/offerings/early-childhood-care-and-education) and [NCERT NCF-FS 2022](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/NCF_for_Foundational_Stage_20_October_2022.pdf).
- **School Years (Classes 1-12):** Individual year plans across [CBSE](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html), [CISCE (ICSE/ISC)](https://www.ciseresults.org/publications.html), [NIOS (OBE, Secondary, Sr. Secondary)](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/), and [State Boards](https://www.mahahsscboard.in/en/studentCorner), anchored in [NCERT NCF-SE 2023](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/NCFSE-2023-August_2023.pdf) and deepened across CBSE Middle Stage (Classes 6-8: *Ganita Prakash*, *Curiosity*, *Exploring Society: India and Beyond*, *Poorvi*, *Malhar*, *Kaushal Bodh*).
- **Competitive & Study-Abroad Tracks (from Class 8-9):** Branches off the shared skill graph for [JEE Main](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/document/syllabus-2026/), [NEET UG](https://www.nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20260108180635.pdf), [CUET UG](https://cuet.nta.nic.in/cuetug-2026-syllabus/), [UPSC CSE](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/revised-syllabus-scheme), [College Board Digital SAT](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/assessment-framework-for-digital-sat-suite.pdf), ACT, [IELTS Academic](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test), and [ETS TOEFL iBT](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content.html).
