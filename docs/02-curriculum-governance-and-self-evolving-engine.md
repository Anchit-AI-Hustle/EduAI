## 1. How India and Global Testing Bodies Prepare and Revise Each Year's Syllabus

To build an AI-first school that covers a learner's whole education without inventing syllabus claims, the system must model the exact institutional machinery that governs school boards and competitive exams.

### 1.1 Policy and National Curriculum Frameworks (NEP 2020 -> NCF-FS 2022 & NCF-SE 2023)

In India, school curriculum does not start with a textbook or a board circular. It follows a four-tier statutory and academic hierarchy:

1. **National Education Policy (NEP 2020):** Sets the structural architecture, replacing the legacy 10+2 system with the **5+3+3+4 curricular structure**:
   - **Foundational Stage (5 years, ages 3-8):** 3 years of Anganwadi/pre-school (Nursery, LKG, UKG) + Classes 1 and 2, preceded by early stimulation for ages 0-3.
   - **Preparatory Stage (3 years, ages 8-11):** Classes 3 to 5, introducing light textbooks and activity-based discovery.
   - **Middle Stage (3 years, ages 11-14):** Classes 6 to 8, introducing abstract concepts across sciences, mathematics, arts, social sciences, and vocational exposure.
   - **Secondary Stage (4 years, ages 14-18):** Classes 9 to 12 across two phases (Classes 9-10 and Classes 11-12) with multidisciplinary flexibility.
2. **Early Childhood Stimulation and ECCE (MWCD 2024):** For birth to age 3, the Ministry of Women and Child Development publishes [Navchetana (National Framework for Early Childhood Stimulation for Children from Birth to Three Years, 2024)](https://spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/early-childhood-care-and-education/documents), structured around monthly caregiver stimulation activities, sensory-motor milestones, and language interaction. For ages 3 to 6, MWCD publishes [Aadharshila (National Curriculum for Early Childhood Care and Education, 2024)](https://spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/early-childhood-care-and-education/documents) aligned with [NCERT NCF-FS 2022](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/NCF_for_Foundational_Stage_20_October_2022.pdf), organized around five developmental domains (*Panchakosha*): Physical Development (*Sharirik Vikas*), Socio-Emotional and Ethical Development (*Manasik/Samajik Vikas*), Cognitive Development (*Bauddhik Vikas*), Language and Literacy Development, and Aesthetic and Cultural Development.
3. **National Curriculum Frameworks ([NCF-FS 2022](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/NCF_for_Foundational_Stage_20_October_2022.pdf) and [NCF-SE 2023](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/NCFSE-2023-August_2023.pdf)):** Developed by [NCERT](https://ncf.ncert.gov.in/) through National Focus Groups, state consultations, and citizen surveys. NCF-SE 2023 defines **Curricular Goals (CG)**, **Competencies (C)**, and **Learning Outcomes (LO)** for every stage, plus time allocation and the [National Credit Framework (NCrF)](https://www.ugc.gov.in/pdfnews/9028476_Report-of-National-Credit-Framework.pdf) standard of **1,200 notional learning hours per year = 40 credits**.
4. **National Syllabus and Teaching-Learning Material Committee ([NSTC](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/notice/Constitution_of_High-Powered_Committee-NSTC.pdf)):** Constituted by NCERT (and updated via [NSTC Reconstitution 2026](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/announcement/Re_constitution_High_Powered_Committee_NSTC.pdf) and [PIB Release on NEP Alignment](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2297094&reg=48&lang=1)) with Curricular Area Groups (CAGs) to translate NCF-SE competencies into concrete syllabi and new NCERT textbooks (such as *Mridang*, *Santoor*, *Ganita Prakash*, *Curiosity*, *Exploring Society: India and Beyond*).

### 1.2 How NCERT and Boards Decide What Changes Are Required (The 5 Rationalisation Criteria)

When NCERT and school boards revise or rationalise a syllabus, they apply five explicit criteria:
1. **Horizontal Overlap:** Content that duplicates material already taught in another subject within the same class.
2. **Vertical Overlap:** Content that repeats material already covered in a lower or higher class in the same subject without adding new competency depth.
3. **Difficulty Level:** Topics whose cognitive load exceeds the age-typical developmental stage or notional hour budget for that grade.
4. **Self-Learning Accessibility:** Material that students can easily access through project work or peer learning without heavy classroom lecturing.
5. **Contextual Relevance:** Material that has become outdated due to scientific, technological, legal, or economic changes.

## 2. How Our AI Self-Evolving System Operates (Five Speeds, Five Safety Gates)

A school that "evolves every minute" must separate **what should adapt every minute** (the learner's mastery model, pacing, and explanation variants) from **what must be version-pinned and human-approved** (the official board syllabus a student's exam depends on).

Our architecture implements a **Five-Layer Self-Evolving Pipeline** (`content/evolution/self-evolving-engine-state.json`):

| Layer | Name | Cadence | What Evolves Automatically | Safety Gate & Governance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | **Real-Time Learner Mastery & Rebalancer** | **Every minute / Every interaction** | Updates per-skill mastery probability (`0.0` to `1.0`), adjusts daily hint depth, and rebalances the current week's schedule when a learner finishes early, struggles on an exit check, or misses a day. | Constrained by topological prerequisite order and daily screen-time cap ([IAP Guidelines](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34969943/)). Never drops a board-mandated `must-do` skill without flagging a mentor. |
| **L2** | **Continuous Pedagogical & Item Optimization** | **Hourly / Daily telemetry loop** | Tracks item discrimination, hint effectiveness, time-to-mastery, and student confusion flags across bilingual (Hindi/English) explanation variants. Promotes high-gain explanation variants and quarantines ambiguous practice items. | Every variant must cite an existing `sourceId` in `official-source-catalog.json`. Automatic rollback if post-lesson exit-check accuracy drops by >5%. |
| **L3** | **Automated Official Source Watch** | **Scheduled Crawler / Feed Monitor** | Polls official endpoints ([CBSE Academic](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html), [NCERT](https://ncert.nic.in/), [CISCE](https://cisce.org/), [NIOS](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/), [NTA](https://nta.ac.in/), [UPSC](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/revised-syllabus-scheme), [College Board](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test), [ETS](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content.html)) for new circulars, syllabus PDF hashes, or exam pattern updates. | Read-only snapshot and diff generator. L3 never mutates live learner plans directly; it emits a structured `SourceDiffEvent` into L4. |
| **L4** | **Cohort-Pinned Curriculum Alignment Gate** | **Triggered on L3 Diff Detection** | Synthesizes a formal **Curriculum Change Proposal** mapping added, deleted, or re-weighted topics onto the shared skill graph, identifying affected cohorts (e.g., Class 10 Batch 2027 vs Batch 2028). | Must pass 4 automated checks before human sign-off: (1) DAG cycle & orphan check, (2) [NCrF](https://www.ugc.gov.in/pdfnews/9028476_Report-of-National-Credit-Framework.pdf) 1,200-hour annual load check, (3) NCERT 5-point rationalisation check, and (4) cohort version pinning + parent changelog publication. |
| **L5** | **Frontier Skill Horizon & Career Bridge** | **Weekly Signal Scan / Quarterly Release** | Adds optional enrichment micro-skills (AI literacy, computational thinking, climate systems, financial literacy, emerging global exam formats) as elective enrichment nodes on the skill graph. | Strictly tagged as `frontier-enrichment` so it never inflates or corrupts compulsory board exam preparation. |
